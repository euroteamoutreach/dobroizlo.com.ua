# Good and Evil Distributor Network

> **Purpose:** This document defines the marketing and terms-of-service content for the Good and Evil Distributor Network operated by Euro Team Outreach, Inc. It covers: (A) what the Distributor Network is and how it works, (B) what we expect from distributors, (C) the onboarding agreement flow for the distributor platform, and (D) content placement across the static marketing site (dobroizlo.com.ua) and the distributor platform (app.dobroizlo.com.ua). Final copy will be translated into Ukrainian.
>
> **Source material:** Google Form "Замовлення книги «Добро і зло» для Вашого служіння" (disabled), project PRD documents.

## A. About the Program

### What Is This?

The Christian mission "Bible First" (Euro Team Outreach, Inc.) provides free copies of the book _Good and Evil_ ("Добро і зло") to individuals and organizations in Ukraine for the purpose of distribution. Eligible participants include churches, missionaries, Christian missions, schools, and community organizations, as well as individuals with a clear plan for distribution.

### How It Works

1. **Register as a distributor.** Create an account and provide information about yourself and your organization. Registration is free. After your account is approved, you'll receive a unique distributor number for future reference.

2. **Plan a distribution event.** Tell us where and how you plan to distribute the books — whether that's a church service, a street outreach, an aid station, a school event, or another setting. Include the city or region and a brief description of your plan.

3. **Request books.** Specify how many books you need for your event. Books are shipped in boxes of 18.

4. **Receive your shipment.** Once your event is approved, we'll ship the books to you via Nova Poshta or Ukrposhta. We can only ship to destinations within Ukraine — international shipping is not available. Please also be aware that due to the ongoing war, some locations within Ukraine may be difficult or impossible to reach safely. We will do our best to ship to every approved event, but in some cases we may not be able to reach your requested destination. You are responsible for covering the shipping costs.

5. **Distribute the books.** Hand out the books at your event. All books must be given away completely free of charge — selling is not permitted.

6. **Submit your report.** After your event, submit a brief report describing how it went, along with 5–10 quality photos. This helps us share the impact of the Distributor Network with our supporters and plan for future print runs.

7. **Request again.** Once your report is submitted and your event is marked as complete, you're welcome to plan your next event and request more books.

### Who Can Participate?

The Distributor Network is open to anyone in Ukraine with a genuine plan to distribute _Good and Evil_ in their community. This includes:

- Churches and church plants
- Missionaries and mission organizations
- Schools and educational institutions
- Community organizations and NGOs
- Individuals engaged in personal outreach

### How Much Does It Cost?

The books themselves are provided free of charge. Distributors are responsible only for covering the shipping costs for the books they receive.

### Registration Is Required

To participate in the Distributor Network, you must register for an account through our online distributor platform. This is the only way to become an official distributor with Euro Team Outreach. All book requests, shipment tracking, and event reporting are managed through the platform. While you're always welcome to reach out with questions via email or messaging apps, the platform is where the network lives — you cannot receive books or function as a distributor without a registered account.

## B. Distributor Expectations

By registering as a distributor and requesting books, you agree to the following:

### Books Must Be Distributed Free of Charge

All copies of _Good and Evil_ that you receive must be distributed absolutely free. You may not sell, resell, or charge any fee for the books under any circumstances.

### You Are Responsible for Shipping Costs

While the books are free, you are responsible for paying the full cost of shipping your books to your location. Shipping is handled through Nova Poshta or Ukrposhta.

### Distribute Books Within 30 Days

Books must be distributed within 30 days of receipt. If you anticipate a delay, please contact us to discuss your situation. We need books to reach people, not sit in storage.

### Submit a Report with Photos

After distributing the books, you are required to submit a report that includes a brief description of how the distribution went and 5–10 quality photos from the event. Photos must be uploaded through the platform as part of your event report. This is the only accepted method for submitting photos.

#### Why Photos Matter

Photos are essential for accountability to the donors who make this network possible. They help us show supporters the real impact of their generosity and demonstrate that books are reaching people across Ukraine. Good photos also help us plan future print runs and inspire others to get involved.

#### A Note on Safety and Privacy

We understand that distribution often takes place in the context of war, and that brings real limitations on photography. We do not expect anyone to take or submit a photo that would violate someone's privacy, threaten security, or be in any way illegal. If your event takes place in a setting where photography is restricted — such as a military hospital, a security-sensitive location, or a situation where participants cannot be identified — please do your best with the photos you are able to take, and explain the circumstances in your report. We would rather receive a few careful photos with context than pressure anyone into a situation that compromises safety.

#### Tips for Good Photos

You don't need professional equipment — a smartphone is perfectly fine. Here are some ideas for the kinds of photos we love to see:

**The distribution in action.** Show the process: unpacking the books, setting them out on a table, handing them to participants. A photo of an event organizer standing before the group explaining the book is great.

**Group photos.** When possible, a group photo of participants holding up their new books and smiling is one of the most impactful images we can share. This works especially well at camps, church events, and school visits.

**Happy faces.** Smiling faces make for wonderful photos, but they're not required. Don't pressure anyone who is uncomfortable being photographed.

**Your team.** Photos of the distribution team — preparing for the event, unloading boxes, or posing with the books before things get started — help tell the full story.

**Creative angles for sensitive settings.** If participants can't be identified in photos, consider shots taken from behind, photos of hands receiving a book, or wider shots where individual faces aren't distinguishable. A photo of a book being handed to someone without showing their face can still be a powerful image.

**The setting itself.** A photo of the venue, the table setup, or even the stack of books before distribution begins helps give context to your event, even if you aren't able to photograph the participants.

### Provide Accurate Information

All information you provide — including your contact details, organization information, and event descriptions — must be complete and accurate. Providing false or misleading information may result in your account being suspended.

### No Intermediaries or Redistribution

Every distributor must register directly with us and request books specifically for their own event. You may not request books on behalf of another person or organization, and you may not pass books along to others for them to distribute separately. If someone you know wants to distribute _Good and Evil_, they are welcome to register as a distributor themselves — we'd love to have them! But each distributor must have their own account, their own event, and their own direct relationship with us. This helps us track where books go, ensure accountability, and serve every distributor well.

Additionally, books are meant to be handed out directly to individual recipients — one or two copies at a time. You and your team should be the ones personally distributing the books at your event. Passing a large quantity of books to another group or organization for them to distribute on their own is not permitted, even if the intent is good. If you know of another group that could use the books for their own event, please encourage them to register as a distributor so we can work with them directly.

**Example:** Your church is hosting a children's camp and you request books to hand out to the kids — that's exactly how the network is meant to work. But if you know someone else who is running a children's camp and you pass your books to them to hand out at their event, that's not permitted. That person or organization should register as a distributor and request books for their own event directly.

### Approval Is Not Guaranteed

Submitting a request does not guarantee that books will be provided. The management of Euro Team Outreach, Inc. makes the final decision on all book requests based on available inventory and network priorities.

### Do Not Alter the Books

Books must be distributed in the condition they were received. You may not physically alter the books in any way — this includes removing pages, stamping or writing on pages, or gluing, taping, or pasting any materials into the book.

If you'd like to share your organization's contact information along with the books, that's perfectly fine. You're welcome to include a loose insert such as a pamphlet, flyer, or business card inside the book so that it's handed out together. The key distinction is that the insert must be a separate, removable item — not permanently attached to the book.

### If You Don't Follow the Rules

We want this network to work well for everyone, and we trust our distributors to act in good faith. If we become aware of a violation of these terms, how we respond depends on the nature and severity of the issue.

**For minor issues** — such as a missed deadline or an incomplete report — we'll typically reach out to discuss the situation and give you a chance to resolve it. We understand that things don't always go according to plan, and we're happy to work with you.

**For more serious violations** — such as unauthorized physical alterations to the books or redistribution to third parties — your account may be suspended while we investigate and discuss the matter with you. Depending on the severity, this could result in a permanent ban from the network.

**For the most serious violations** — specifically, selling books that were provided to you for free distribution — your account will be permanently terminated. This is non-negotiable. The books are donated by supporters who give specifically so that they can be distributed at no cost. Selling them is a betrayal of that trust.

Euro Team Outreach reserves the right to suspend or terminate any distributor account at its discretion.

### We're Paying Attention

Once your books have been shipped, we'll be following up. Our team tracks the status of every event and every shipment. If your books have been delivered and we haven't heard from you, we will reach out. We expect our distributors to stay in communication with us, complete their events in a timely manner, and submit their reports promptly. Remember, you cannot request more books until your current event is complete — and that means submitting your report with photos.

### One Active Event at a Time

You may have one active event at a time. To request books for a new event, your previous event must be completed — meaning your report has been submitted and reviewed.

## C. Onboarding Agreement Flow

Rather than presenting the full terms of service as a single document with a generic "I agree" checkbox, the onboarding process should walk new distributors through a series of brief, focused commitment statements — each requiring individual acknowledgment. This approach mirrors the effective pattern used in the original Google Form and ensures that distributors actually read and understand the key expectations before they can begin requesting books.

### Design Principles

- Each statement should be short enough to read in a few seconds — two or three sentences at most.
- Each statement requires its own checkbox or confirmation action.
- Statements are presented one at a time (or in small groups of 2–3) so that the user's attention is focused.
- The experience should feel like a conversation, not a legal proceeding. The tone should be consistent with the rest of our content — clear, warm, and direct.
- At the end, the user is offered a link to the complete terms of service and asked to confirm that they agree to be bound by them.

### Proposed Commitment Statements

The following statements represent the most critical expectations. Each one maps to a specific rule in Section B.

**1. Free distribution only**
"All copies of _Good and Evil_ that you receive through the Distributor Network must be distributed completely free of charge. You may not sell or charge any fee for the books."

**2. Shipping costs**
"The books are provided free, but you are responsible for paying the full cost of shipping to your location."

**3. Distribute within 30 days**
"You commit to distributing the books within 30 days of receiving your shipment. If you expect a delay, please contact us."

**4. Submit a report with photos**
"After your distribution event, you are required to submit a report with 5–10 photos through the platform. This is essential for accountability to the donors who fund this network."

**5. Direct distribution only — no intermediaries**
"You must personally distribute the books at your own event. You may not pass books to another person or organization for them to distribute separately. Books are to be handed out to individual recipients, one or two at a time."

**6. Do not alter the books**
"Books must be distributed in the condition they were received. You may not remove pages, stamp or write on them, or permanently attach any materials to the books."

**7. Accurate information**
"All information you provide — including your contact details, organization information, and event descriptions — must be complete and accurate."

### Final Acknowledgment

After the individual statements, a final screen presents a summary and a link to the complete terms:

"These are the key commitments of the _Good and Evil_ Distributor Network. The complete terms of service, including our photo guidelines, enforcement policies, and other details, are available here: [link to full terms]. By continuing, you confirm that you have read and agree to be bound by these terms as a participant in the Distributor Network."

This final step uses a single checkbox or confirmation button to complete the onboarding agreement.

### Implementation Notes

- The agreement must be completed once during onboarding, before the distributor can create their first event.
- The date and time of agreement should be recorded on the distributor's account for reference.
- If the terms are updated in the future, returning distributors may need to re-acknowledge the updated terms before creating a new event. This is a future consideration and does not need to be built for MVP.
- The individual commitment statements and the final acknowledgment should be translatable via i18n, consistent with the app's bilingual approach.

## D. Content Placement

### Two Sites, Two Purposes

The content in this document is split across two sites that serve distinct but related functions:

**dobroizlo.com.ua** (static marketing site) is the primary entry point for anyone discovering _Good and Evil_. Its main purpose is to offer individual Ukrainians a single personal copy of the book. However, it also serves as the gateway to the Distributor Network for visitors who want to get more involved. The static site should include a dedicated page — something like "Become a Distributor" or "Join the Distributor Network" — that provides a warm, inviting overview of the network: what it is, who it's for, and how it works at a high level. This page draws from Section A of this document but does not include the detailed terms or obligations. Its goal is to spark interest and direct people to the app with a clear call to action.

**app.dobroizlo.com.ua** (distributor platform) is where the Distributor Network lives. This is the authoritative home for the full terms and expectations outlined in Section B of this document. Distributors encounter these terms during onboarding — after creating their account but before they can submit their first event. The app is the only place where someone can register as a distributor, request books, track shipments, and submit reports.

### Navigation Between the Sites

The static marketing site should present two clear paths to visitors:

- **Primary path:** "Want a copy of Good and Evil for yourself?" — this leads to the individual request form, which is the site's main focus.
- **Secondary path:** "Want to distribute Good and Evil in your community?" — this leads to the Distributor Network overview page, which in turn links to the app for registration.

The secondary path does not need to compete with the primary call to action. It can be presented as a supporting section on the homepage, a link in the navigation, a mention in the footer, or a combination — whatever feels natural without cluttering the main message. The key is that it's discoverable for people who are looking for more than just a personal copy.

### What Goes Where

| Content | Static Site | App |
| --------- | ------------ | ----- |
| What is the Distributor Network? | High-level overview (Section A, condensed) | Full version during onboarding |
| How does it work? | Step-by-step summary | Reflected in the app's actual workflow |
| Who can participate? | Brief eligibility summary | Full details during onboarding |
| Distributor expectations and obligations | Not included | Full terms (Section B) presented during onboarding |
| Photo guidelines | Not included | Shown during event report submission |
| Enforcement and consequences | Not included | Part of the terms accepted during onboarding |

## E. Internal Feature Notes

The following items came up during content development and need to be addressed in the ComixDistro PRD or app backlog. They are not public-facing content.

### Overdue Event Tracking and Admin Reminders

The app needs a mechanism to track events that are running behind schedule. When books have been delivered and the 30-day distribution window is passing without a submitted report, admins need to be alerted. This should surface on the admin dashboard — something like a list of overdue or at-risk events showing the distributor name, delivery date, days elapsed, and last communication. Automated reminder emails to the distributor may also be appropriate (e.g., a gentle nudge at 21 days, a firmer reminder at 30 days). This ties into the existing notification infrastructure in `08-notifications.md`.

### Banned Distributor Registry

When a distributor account is permanently terminated, the app needs to retain a record of the associated information — email addresses, phone numbers, organization name, contact names, and any relevant notes about the reason for the ban. This serves as a reference when new distributor registrations come in, allowing admins to check whether an applicant matches a previously banned individual or organization. This could be as simple as a "banned" status on the Distributor model with preserved data and admin notes, plus a flagging mechanism during new registration review.

### Ukrposhta Shipping Support

The app currently only supports Nova Poshta for shipments. The Google Form offered Ukrposhta as an alternative (address-based delivery instead of depot selection). This needs to be added to the shipment model — likely a `shipping_method` field that drives whether the depot or full postal address fields are required. See `06-shipments-and-inventory.md`.
