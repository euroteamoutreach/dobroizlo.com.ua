# Good and Evil — Naming & Branding Guide

> This document is the authoritative naming and branding reference for all digital properties associated with the book «Добро і зло» (*Good and Evil*), operated by Euro Team Outreach, Inc. It governs terminology, naming conventions, and branding patterns across the marketing site (dobroizlo.com.ua), the distributor platform (app.dobroizlo.com.ua), transactional emails, and any future Good-and-Evil-related applications or services. All implementers — including developers, translators, and AI coding assistants — should treat this document as the standard and build against it.

## Core Naming Conventions

### The Book

- **Full (Ukrainian):** Біблія-комікс «Добро і зло»
- **Full (English):** the *Good and Evil* book
- **Short form (when context is established):** «Добро і зло» / *Good and Evil*
- **Guideline:** Always use at least one contextual word (книга, комікс, Біблія-комікс) alongside the title when addressing someone who might not know what it is. "Біблія-комікс" is the preferred qualifier because it conveys both content (Bible) and format (comic) in two words. When context is already established (e.g., within the app, mid-conversation with a distributor), the title alone is fine.

### The Network

- **Formal name (Ukrainian):** Мережа дистриб'юторів «Добро і зло»
- **Formal name (English):** Good and Evil Distributor Network
- **Standard (Ukrainian):** Мережа дистриб'юторів
- **Short branded form (Ukrainian):** Мережа «Добро і зло»
- **Short branded form (English):** Good and Evil Network

### Naming Pattern

The standard Ukrainian naming pattern is **Qualifier «Title»** — a generic noun or descriptor followed by the proper name in guillemets. This follows established Ukrainian convention (e.g., Церква «Більша благодать», ресторан «Пузата хата»). There is no dash or other punctuation between the qualifier and the guillemeted name. This pattern applies across all contexts unless a specific exception is noted below.

## Application by Context

- **Browser title tag:** Біблія-комікс «Добро і зло» (qualifier first, then title)

- **Nav link to distributor page:** Дистриб'юторам
- **Distributor page heading:** Мережа дистриб'юторів

### Distributor App (app.dobroizlo.com.ua)

- **Sidebar / navbar brand:** Діз logo + Добро і зло (bare title — matches the marketing site; context is already established for logged-in users)
- **Login and signup pages:** Display the logo lockup (open book image + "Мережа дистриб'юторів «Добро і зло»") — these are first-contact surfaces where the qualifier helps orient new visitors

### Email "From:" Field

- **For distributor-facing emails:** Мережа «Добро і зло»
- **For general/individual-request emails:** Біблія-комікс «Добро і зло»
- **Guideline:** Use the Qualifier «Title» pattern in both cases; swap the qualifier based on audience.

### Transactional Email Header

- **Primary line:** Мережа дистриб'юторів
- **Secondary line:** «Добро і зло»
- **Layout:** Book cover thumbnail or open book image on the left, text on the right, thin horizontal rule below, then email body.

- **Footer attribution:** Мережа дистриб'юторів «Добро і зло» — служіння Euro Team Outreach, Inc. (full formal name — the footer is a legal context where maximum explicitness is appropriate)

### Logo Text

- **Ukrainian:** Мережа дистриб'юторів «Добро і зло» (network name leads, book title qualifies)
- **English:** Good and Evil Distributor Network (book title leads — acceptable for internal/supporter-facing contexts)

## Vocabulary Standards

- **Event:** Use **подія** consistently across the app and emails to refer to a distributor's distribution event. Do not mix with "захід" or other synonyms.
- **Network vs. program:** Always use **Мережа** (network), never "програма" (program). "Мережа" implies ongoing collaboration rather than a one-time sign-up.
- **Bare title in navigation:** Within the app and the marketing site, the navbar uses the bare title "Добро і зло" alongside the Діз logo mark. This is acceptable because the user is already in context. Qualified forms (with Біблія-комікс, Мережа, etc.) are reserved for first-contact surfaces where the user may not yet know what this is.
- **книга vs. книжка:** Both are correct Ukrainian words for "book," but they differ in register. **Книга** is the standard, neutral term — use it in the distributor app, transactional emails, terms of service, and any formal or institutional context. **Книжка** is the colloquial, warmer form — use it on the marketing site and in informal communications where the tone is conversational. Do not mix registers within the same context.

## Ukrainian Grammar Rules

### Title Declension

The title «Добро і зло» must always appear in guillemets and always in the **nominative case**, regardless of the grammatical case required by the surrounding sentence. This is standard Ukrainian practice for all proper titles enclosed in guillemets. When a case relationship is needed, place a classifier noun before the guillemeted title and decline the classifier instead. The title itself never changes form.

- **Correct:** примірник «Добро і зло» (classifier "примірник" carries the case relationship)
- **Correct:** книги «Добро і зло» ("книги" declines to genitive, title stays nominative)
- **Incorrect:** примірник Добра і зла (treats the title words as common nouns and declines them)

This rule applies everywhere — page headings, email copy, UI text, marketing content. If a sentence would require "Добро і зло" to change form, restructure to use a classifier noun or rephrase the sentence. Never decline the words inside the guillemets.
